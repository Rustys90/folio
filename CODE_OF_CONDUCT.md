# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (abridged)

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Folio a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- Be kind, respectful, and constructive.
- Welcome newcomers; assume good intent.
- No harassment, insults, or exclusionary behavior — in issues, PRs, chat, or anywhere
  attached to this project.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to the maintainers, who will review and respond. The full
[Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
applies; this file is a plain-language summary of it.
